About Me

Engineering Knowledge. Educational Impact. Intelligent Innovation.

I am Ann Amarachukwu Odimegwu, a Lecturer II in Industrial Technical Education at the University of Nigeria, Nsukka, and a doctoral researcher with over 15 years of experience spanning vocational pedagogy, electrical and electronic systems, and renewable energy.

My professional journey combines engineering expertise, technical device design, research, statistical analysis and the development of practical approaches to technical education.
